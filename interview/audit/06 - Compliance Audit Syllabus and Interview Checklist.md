## 📌 Compliance Framework Quick Reference

| Framework | Focus Area | Region |
|---|---|---|
| ISO/IEC 27001 | Information Security Management System | Global |
| NIST CSF | Cybersecurity risk management | US (widely adopted globally) |
| GDPR | Personal data privacy | EU (extraterritorial effect) |
| PCI DSS | Payment card data security | Global (card industry) |
| HIPAA | Healthcare data privacy | US |
| SOX | Financial reporting controls | US (public companies) |
| CIS Controls | Prioritized technical safeguards | Global |
| DPDP Act 2023 | Personal data protection | India |
