# Ethical Hacking

## What is Ethical Hacking?

Ethical hacking is the **authorized and legal process** of testing computer systems, networks, applications, or organizations to identify security weaknesses before malicious attackers exploit them.

Ethical hackers act like real attackers but work with **permission** to improve security.

### Simple Definition

> Ethical hacking = legally breaking into systems to find and fix vulnerabilities.

---

# Objectives of Ethical Hacking

* Identify vulnerabilities
* Test security defenses
* Prevent cyber attacks
* Protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability
* Improve overall security posture

---

# Phases of Ethical Hacking

## 1. Reconnaissance (Information Gathering)

Collect information about the target.

### Types

* Passive Reconnaissance
* Active Reconnaissance

### Tools

* WHOIS
* Google Dorking
* Nmap
* Maltego

---

## 2. Scanning

Identify:

* Open ports
* Services
* Operating systems
* Vulnerabilities

### Tools

* Nmap
* Nessus
* OpenVAS

---

## 3. Gaining Access

Attempt to exploit vulnerabilities.

### Examples

* SQL Injection
* Password attacks
* Exploit vulnerabilities

### Tools

* Metasploit
* Burp Suite

---

## 4. Maintaining Access

Check whether attackers can remain inside the system.

### Examples

* Privilege escalation
* Persistence testing

---

## 5. Clearing Tracks

Attackers may hide activities by deleting logs.

Ethical hackers study these techniques to improve defenses.

## Q2. Why Are Insider Threats Dangerous?

Because insiders:

* Already have access
* Know internal systems
* Can bypass some security controls

## Q3. Are Grey Hat Hackers Illegal?

Usually yes, because they may access systems without permission even if intentions are not malicious.
