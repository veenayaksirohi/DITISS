# 1. International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 2700x Family

## What is ISO?

International Organization for Standardization (ISO) is an independent international organization that develops standards to ensure quality, safety, efficiency, and interoperability across industries.

### Key Facts

* Established: 1947
* Headquarters: Geneva
* Members: National standards bodies from over 160 countries
* Publishes international standards

---

## What is the ISO 27000 Series?

The ISO 27000 family focuses on **Information Security Management Systems (ISMS)**.

Purpose:

* Protect Confidentiality
* Protect Integrity
* Protect Availability

(CIA Triad)

---

## Important ISO 2700x Standards

| Standard  | Purpose                               |
| --------- | ------------------------------------- |
| ISO 27000 | Overview and vocabulary               |
| ISO 27001 | Requirements for ISMS                 |
| ISO 27002 | Security controls guidelines          |
| ISO 27003 | ISMS implementation guidance          |
| ISO 27004 | Security measurement                  |
| ISO 27005 | Risk management                       |
| ISO 27017 | Cloud security                        |
| ISO 27018 | Privacy protection in cloud           |
| ISO 27701 | Privacy Information Management System |

# 2. How the Standard Works

ISO 27001 follows a systematic approach for managing sensitive information.

---

## Step 1: Establish ISMS

Identify:

* Information assets
* Risks
* Security requirements
* Legal obligations

---

## Step 2: Risk Assessment

Determine:

* Threats
* Vulnerabilities
* Impact
* Likelihood

Risk Formula:

[
Risk = Impact \times Likelihood
]

---

## Step 3: Risk Treatment

Options:

1. Mitigate
2. Transfer
3. Accept
4. Avoid

---

## Step 4: Implement Controls

Controls selected from ISO 27001 Annex A.

Examples:

* Access Control
* Password Policy
* Backup
* Encryption
* Incident Response

---

## Step 5: Monitor and Improve

Use:

* Internal audits
* Management review
* Corrective actions

---

## PDCA Cycle (Core Concept)

### Plan

* Define policies
* Assess risks

### Do

* Implement controls

### Check

* Audit performance

### Act

* Improve system

---

### Easy Memory Trick

**PDCA = Plan → Do → Check → Act**

---

# 3. History of ISO/IEC 27001

## Origins

### 1995

British Standard:

**BS 7799**

Developed by:

British Standards Institution

---

### 1999

BS 7799 split into:

* BS 7799 Part 1
* BS 7799 Part 2

---

### 2000

BS 7799 Part 1 became:

ISO/IEC 17799

---

### 2005

ISO/IEC 27001:2005 released

First official ISMS certification standard.

---

### 2013

Major revision:

ISO/IEC 27001:2013

Introduced:

* Annex SL structure
* Risk-based approach

---

### 2022

Current version:

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Updated control structure.

---

## Exam Timeline Trick

```
1995 → BS 7799
2000 → ISO 17799
2005 → ISO 27001
2013 → Major Update
2022 → Latest Version
```

# 4. ISO/IEC 27001:2005 Domains

ISO 27001:2005 contained **11 Security Domains**.

These are frequently asked in exams.

---

## Domain 1: Security Policy

Defines security direction.

Example:

* Information Security Policy

---

## Domain 2: Organization of Information Security

Defines roles and responsibilities.

Example:

* Security Officer

---

## Domain 3: Asset Management

Protect organizational assets.

Examples:

* Laptops
* Databases
* Documents

---

## Domain 4: Human Resources Security

Security before, during, and after employment.

Examples:

* Background verification
* Awareness training

---

## Domain 5: Physical and Environmental Security

Protect physical assets.

Examples:

* CCTV
* Biometric access
* UPS

---

## Domain 6: Communications and Operations Management

Secure daily operations.

Examples:

* Backup
* Logging
* Malware protection

---

## Domain 7: Access Control

Control access to systems.

Examples:

* Passwords
* MFA
* RBAC

---

## Domain 8: Information Systems Acquisition, Development and Maintenance

Secure software development.

Examples:

* Secure coding
* Testing

---

## Domain 9: Information Security Incident Management

Manage security incidents.

Examples:

* Reporting breaches
* Response procedures

---

## Domain 10: Business Continuity Management

Ensure operations continue during disruptions.

Examples:

* Disaster Recovery
* BCP

---

## Domain 11: Compliance

Meet legal and regulatory requirements.

Examples:

* GDPR
* Contracts
* Audits

# 5. Structure of ISO/IEC 27001 Standard

ISO 27001 follows High-Level Structure (HLS).

---

## Clause 1: Scope

Defines applicability.

---

## Clause 2: Normative References

Supporting standards.

---

## Clause 3: Terms and Definitions

Security terminology.

---

## Clause 4: Context of Organization

Understand:

* Internal issues
* External issues
* Stakeholders

---

## Clause 5: Leadership

Top management commitment.

---

## Clause 6: Planning

Risk assessment and treatment.

---

## Clause 7: Support

Resources and awareness.

---

## Clause 8: Operation

Implement ISMS.

---

## Clause 9: Performance Evaluation

* Monitoring
* Audits
* Reviews

---

## Clause 10: Improvement

Corrective actions and continual improvement.

---
