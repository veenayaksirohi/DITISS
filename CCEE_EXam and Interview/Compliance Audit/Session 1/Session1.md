# Cyber Security Audit & Compliance – Complete Exam Notes

---

# 1. Introduction to Security Audit

A **Security Audit** is a systematic evaluation of an organization's information systems, policies, procedures, and controls to determine whether security requirements are being met.

### Objectives

* Identify vulnerabilities and risks
* Verify compliance with standards and regulations
* Evaluate effectiveness of security controls
* Protect Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA)
* Improve overall security posture

### Importance

* Prevent cyber attacks
* Ensure legal compliance
* Protect organizational assets
* Build customer trust
* Reduce financial losses

### Key Terms

| Term          | Meaning                        |
| ------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Audit         | Formal examination of controls |
| Compliance    | Following standards/laws       |
| Risk          | Potential loss due to threat   |
| Threat        | Possible danger                |
| Vulnerability | Weakness in system             |
| Control       | Safeguard to reduce risk       |

---

# 2. Cyber Security Challenges for Organizations

Organizations face multiple cybersecurity challenges.

## 1. Increasing Cyber Attacks

* Malware
* Ransomware
* Phishing
* DDoS attacks

## 2. Insider Threats

Employees may:

* Accidentally leak data
* Intentionally steal information

## 3. Cloud Security Risks

* Misconfigured storage
* Weak access controls
* Data leakage

## 4. Remote Work Security

* Unsecured home networks
* Personal device usage
* VPN misuse

## 5. Third-Party Risks

* Vendors
* Suppliers
* Outsourced services

## 6. Compliance Requirements

Need to follow:

* ISO 27001
* PCI-DSS
* GDPR
* HIPAA

## 7. Skill Shortage

Lack of trained security professionals.

---

# 3. Compliance Basics

Compliance means adhering to laws, regulations, standards, and organizational policies.

## Why Compliance?

### Legal Requirement

Avoid penalties and lawsuits.

### Security Improvement

Provides structured security framework.

### Customer Confidence

Builds trust.

### Risk Reduction

Minimizes chances of breaches.

---

## Common Compliance Standards

### ISO 27001

Information Security Management System (ISMS)

### PCI-DSS

Protects payment card information.

### GDPR

Protects personal data of EU citizens.

### HIPAA

Protects healthcare information.

### SOC 2

Security and privacy assurance.

---

### Compliance Process

```text
Identify Requirements
        ↓
Implement Controls
        ↓
Monitor Compliance
        ↓
Audit
        ↓
Continuous Improvement
```

# 4. Types of Security Audit

## 1. Internal Audit

Performed by organization's own audit team.

### Advantages

* Low cost
* Better understanding of organization

### Disadvantages

* Potential bias

---

## 2. External Audit

Performed by independent third party.

### Advantages

* Objective assessment
* Higher credibility

### Disadvantages

* Expensive

---

## 3. Compliance Audit

Checks compliance with regulations.

Examples:

* ISO 27001
* PCI-DSS

---

## 4. Technical Audit

Focuses on:

* Networks
* Servers
* Firewalls
* Applications

---

## 5. Operational Audit

Reviews operational effectiveness.

---

## 6. Risk-Based Audit

Focuses on high-risk areas first.

---

# 5. Audit Decision Factors

Before conducting an audit, several factors are considered.

## Risk Level

Higher risk = Higher audit priority

## Business Criticality

Critical systems audited more frequently.

## Regulatory Requirements

Mandatory audits due to compliance.

## Previous Audit Findings

Unresolved issues require re-audit.

## Organizational Changes

* New software
* New infrastructure
* Mergers

## Security Incidents

Recent breaches increase audit necessity.

---

## Phase 1: Planning

Activities:

* Define scope
* Define objectives
* Identify resources
* Schedule audit

## Phase 2: Information Gathering

* Interviews

* Technical testing

---

## Phase 3: Risk Assessment

Identify:

* Threats
* Vulnerabilities

---

## Phase 4: Evaluation

Compare controls against:

* Policies
* Standards
* Regulations

---

## Phase 5: Reporting

Report includes:

* Findings
* Risks
* Recommendations

## Phase 6: Follow-up

Verify corrective actions.
