# Super Exam Shortcut

**GIPDRR**

**G** → Govern (Policies, Risk Strategy)

**I** → Identify (Assets, Risks)

**P** → Protect (Firewall, MFA, Encryption)

**D** → Detect (Logs, IDS, SIEM)

**R** → Respond (Containment, Mitigation)

**R** → Recover (Backups, DR, BCP)

This mnemonic alone can answer nearly **40–50% of NIST MCQs** in certification, university, and placement exams.
