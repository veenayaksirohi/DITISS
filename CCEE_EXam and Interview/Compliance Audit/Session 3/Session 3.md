# 1. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Overview

## What is NIST?

The National Institute of Standards and Technology is a U.S. government agency under the United States Department of Commerce that develops standards, guidelines, and best practices for information security and risk management.

NIST provides frameworks and controls that help organizations:

* Protect information assets
* Manage cybersecurity risks
* Improve security posture
* Detect and respond to cyber threats
* Recover from security incidents

# 2. Components of NIST Cybersecurity Framework

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework consists of **6 Core Functions**.

---

## 1. Govern (Added in CSF 2.0)

### Purpose

Establish cybersecurity governance and oversight.

### Activities

* Security policies
* Risk management strategy
* Roles and responsibilities
* Compliance requirements

### Example

Organization defines:

* Security policies
* Asset ownership
* Cybersecurity objectives

### Keyword

**Govern = Management and Direction**

---

## 2. Identify

### Purpose

Understand assets, risks, and business environment.

### Activities

* Asset inventory
* Risk assessment
* Business impact analysis
* Resource management

### Example

Identify:

* Servers
* Databases
* Applications
* Critical business processes

### Keyword

**Identify = Know What You Have**

---

## 3. Protect

### Purpose

Implement safeguards to protect assets.

### Activities

* Access control
* Employee awareness training
* Data security
* Encryption
* Firewall configuration

### Example

* Strong passwords
* Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
* Security awareness training

### Keyword

**Protect = Prevent Attacks**

---

## 4. Detect

### Purpose

Identify cybersecurity incidents quickly.

### Activities

* Log monitoring
* Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)
* Security monitoring
* Continuous assessment

### Example

* SIEM alerts
* Snort IDS alerts
* Suspicious login detection

### Keyword

**Detect = Find Attacks**

---

## 5. Respond

### Purpose

Take action after incident detection.

### Activities

* Incident response
* Communication
* Containment
* Analysis
* Mitigation

### Example

* Isolate infected machine
* Block malicious IP address
* Notify stakeholders

### Keyword

**Respond = Handle Attacks**

---

## 6. Recover

### Purpose

Restore services after incident.

### Activities

* Backup restoration
* Disaster recovery
* Business continuity
* Lessons learned

### Example

* Restore server from backup
* Resume operations

### Keyword

**Recover = Return to Normal**

---

# Easy Memorization Trick

## G-I-P-D-R-R

### G → Govern

### I → Identify

### P → Protect

### D → Detect

### R → Respond

### R → Recover

Remember:

**"Good IT Professionals Detect Risks Rapidly"**

# Most Important 5 Marks Answer

**NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)** is a risk-based framework developed by NIST to help organizations manage cybersecurity risks. It consists of six core functions: **Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.** The framework improves security posture, supports compliance, enhances incident response, and helps organizations protect critical assets through continuous risk management.
