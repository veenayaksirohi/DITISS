# Session 9 Notes – COBIT Framework (2T)

# 1. COBIT Framework

### What is COBIT?

**COBIT (Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies)** is an IT Governance and Management Framework developed by the ISACA.

It helps organizations:

* Align IT with business goals
* Manage risks
* Ensure compliance
* Improve governance
* Optimize IT resources
* Measure IT performance

---

## Definition

> COBIT provides a comprehensive framework for governance and management of enterprise information and technology.

---

# Why COBIT is Important

Organizations use COBIT to:

✅ Ensure business and IT alignment

✅ Improve decision making

✅ Manage risks effectively

✅ Achieve regulatory compliance

✅ Increase operational efficiency

✅ Improve security and controls

---

# COBIT Domains

COBIT divides activities into:

## Governance Domain

### EDM

**Evaluate, Direct and Monitor**

Responsibilities:

* Strategic direction
* Risk oversight
* Performance monitoring

---

## Management Domains

### APO

**Align, Plan and Organize**

Examples:

* IT strategy
* Budgeting
* Resource management

---

### BAI

**Build, Acquire and Implement**

Examples:

* Software development
* Change management
* Project management

---

### DSS

**Deliver, Service and Support**

Examples:

* Incident management
* Security management
* Service delivery

---

### MEA

**Monitor, Evaluate and Assess**

Examples:

* Audits
* Compliance checks
* Performance monitoring

---

# 2. COBIT Components

COBIT 2019 uses **7 Components** (formerly called Enablers).

---

## 1. Processes

Activities that achieve objectives.

Examples:

* Change Management
* Incident Management
* Risk Management

---

## 2. Organizational Structures

Decision-making entities.

Examples:

* Board of Directors
* IT Steering Committee
* Security Team

---

## 3. Principles, Policies and Procedures

Guidelines governing operations.

Examples:

* Password Policy
* Backup Policy
* Security Policy

---

## 4. Information

Data used in decision making.

Examples:

* Audit reports
* Logs
* Business reports

---

## 5. Culture, Ethics and Behavior

Human behavior affecting governance.

Examples:

* Security awareness
* Ethical conduct
* Compliance culture

---

## 6. People, Skills and Competencies

Qualified personnel required.

Examples:

* IT Managers
* Security Analysts
* Auditors

---

## 7. Services, Infrastructure and Applications

Technology resources supporting business.

Examples:

* Servers
* Networks
* Applications
* Cloud services

---

# 3. COBIT vs ITIL

---

## What is ITIL?

**ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library)** is a framework for IT Service Management (ITSM).

It focuses on delivering IT services efficiently.

---

# Key Difference

### COBIT

Focuses on:

* Governance
* Control
* Risk
* Compliance

### ITIL

Focuses on:

* Service Management
* Service Delivery
* Customer Satisfaction

---

# COBIT vs ITIL Comparison

| Feature             | COBIT                                                       | ITIL                                          |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Full Form           | Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies | Information Technology Infrastructure Library |
| Focus               | Governance & Management                                     | Service Management                            |
| Developed By        | ISACA                                                       | AXELOS                                        |
| Scope               | Enterprise-wide IT Governance                               | IT Service Management                         |
| Primary Goal        | Align IT with Business                                      | Deliver Quality IT Services                   |
| Risk Management     | Strong Focus                                                | Limited Focus                                 |
| Compliance          | Strong Focus                                                | Moderate Focus                                |
| Auditing            | Supported                                                   | Limited                                       |
| Service Desk        | Not Main Focus                                              | Major Focus                                   |
| Incident Management | Basic                                                       | Detailed                                      |
| Change Management   | Governance View                                             | Operational View                              |
| Users               | Auditors, Managers, Executives                              | Service Managers, IT Operations               |
