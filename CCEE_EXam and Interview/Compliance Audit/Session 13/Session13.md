# 1. Information Technology Act, 2000 (Amended in 2008)

## Introduction

The IT Act, 2000 is India's primary law dealing with:

* Electronic Records
* Electronic Signatures
* Cyber Crimes
* E-Commerce
* Data Protection
* Digital Governance

The IT (Amendment) Act, 2008 strengthened provisions related to cyber security and cyber crime.

---

## Objectives

* Legal recognition of electronic records
* Legal recognition of digital signatures
* Facilitate e-commerce
* Prevent cyber crimes
* Protect information systems

---

## Important Sections

### Section 43

Penalty for unauthorized access to computer systems.

Examples:

* Data theft
* Virus attacks
* Downloading data without permission

---

### Section 66

Computer-related offences.

Punishment:

* Up to 3 years imprisonment
* Fine up to ₹5 lakh

---

### Section 66C

Identity Theft

Examples:

* Password theft
* Using someone else's digital signature

Punishment:

* Up to 3 years imprisonment
* Fine up to ₹1 lakh

---

### Section 66D

Cheating by Personation Using Computer Resources

Examples:

* Online scams
* Phishing attacks

Punishment:

* Up to 3 years imprisonment
* Fine up to ₹1 lakh

---

### Section 66E

Violation of Privacy

Capturing or publishing private images without consent.

---

### Section 66F

Cyber Terrorism

Examples:

* Attacking critical infrastructure
* National security attacks

Punishment:

* Life imprisonment

---

### Section 67

Publishing Obscene Content

Punishment:

* Imprisonment and fine

---

### Section 69

Government power to:

* Intercept
* Monitor
* Decrypt information

For:

* National security
* Public order

---

### Section 72

Breach of Confidentiality and Privacy

Unauthorized disclosure of information.

---

## Digital Signature

Uses:

* Authentication
* Integrity
* Non-repudiation

Based on:

* Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)

---

## Certifying Authorities

Managed by:

Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA)

Responsibilities:

* Issue licenses
* Regulate digital certificates

---

# ITAA 2008 Important Additions

### Introduced

* Cyber terrorism (66F)
* Identity theft (66C)
* Phishing and fraud (66D)
* Data protection obligations
* Intermediary liability provisions

---

# Exam Tip

Remember:

66C → Identity Theft

66D → Phishing/Fraud

66F → Cyber Terrorism

67 → Obscene Content

# 2. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)

## Purpose

Protect personal data of Indian citizens while enabling lawful processing.

---

## Key Terms

### Data Principal

Person whose data is collected.

Example:
Customer

---

### Data Fiduciary

Organization collecting data.

Example:
Bank, Hospital, E-commerce company

---

### Data Processor

Processes data on behalf of fiduciary.

Example:
Cloud service provider

---

## Rights of Data Principal

### Right to Information

Know:

* What data is collected
* Why collected

---

### Right to Correction

Correct inaccurate data.

---

### Right to Erasure

Request deletion of data.

---

### Right to Grievance Redressal

File complaints.

---

### Right to Nominate

Nominate another person to exercise rights.

---

## Consent Requirements

Consent must be:

* Free
* Specific
* Informed
* Unambiguous
* Revocable

---

## Obligations of Data Fiduciary

* Protect data
* Implement security safeguards
* Report breaches
* Delete unnecessary data

---

## Significant Data Fiduciaries (SDF)

Large organizations may be classified as SDF.

Additional requirements:

* Data Protection Officer
* Independent Audits
* Risk Assessments

---

## Data Breach Notification

Must report breaches to:

### Data Protection Board of India

Data Protection Board of India

---

## Penalties

Can reach hundreds of crores depending on violation.

Maximum penalties can exceed ₹250 crore for serious violations.

---

# DPDP Act Quick Revision

| Term           | Meaning                |
| -------------- | ---------------------- |
| Data Principal | Owner of data          |
| Data Fiduciary | Collector of data      |
| Data Processor | Processor of data      |
| Consent        | User permission        |
| SDF            | High-risk organization |

# 3. Case Study – Internal Audit Solution for a Global Bank

## Scenario

A multinational bank operates across multiple countries.

Challenges:

* Regulatory compliance
* Cyber threats
* Fraud detection
* Operational risk

---

## Internal Audit Objectives

* Assess controls
* Ensure compliance
* Reduce fraud
* Improve governance

---

## Audit Areas

### IT Infrastructure

Review:

* Servers
* Databases
* Cloud systems

---

### User Access Management

Check:

* Least privilege
* RBAC
* Privileged accounts

---

### Security Controls

Verify:

* Firewalls
* IDS/IPS
* SIEM
* MFA

---

### Financial Controls

Review:

* Transactions
* Reconciliations
* Fraud monitoring

---

### Regulatory Compliance

Ensure compliance with:

* PCI DSS
* ISO 27001
* GDPR
* Local banking regulations

---

## Audit Findings Example

Issue:
Excessive user privileges.

Risk:
Insider threat.

Recommendation:
Implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

---

## Benefits

* Better risk management
* Improved compliance
* Fraud reduction
* Enhanced customer trust

# 4. Compliance Audit of Anti-Corruption Regulations

## Purpose

Ensure organization complies with anti-bribery laws.

---

## Key Regulations

### Prevention of Corruption Act

### Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA)

### UK Bribery Act

---

## Audit Objectives

Verify:

* No bribery
* No kickbacks
* Proper gift policies
* Ethical conduct

---

## Audit Procedures

### Policy Review

Check:

* Anti-bribery policies
* Code of conduct

---

### Employee Training

Verify:

* Awareness programs
* Compliance certifications

---

### Vendor Due Diligence

Review:

* Third-party relationships
* Supplier background checks

---

### Financial Transaction Testing

Check:

* Suspicious payments
* Unusual expenses
* Cash transactions

---

## Common Findings

* Missing approvals
* Weak monitoring
* Vendor conflicts of interest
* Inadequate documentation

---

## Recommendations

* Stronger controls
* Automated monitoring
* Employee awareness
* Regular audits
