# Purpose of Chain of Custody (CoC)

**Chain of Custody** is used to maintain and document the complete history of evidence from collection to presentation in court.

## Main Purposes

### 1. Preserve Evidence Integrity

Ensures evidence is not altered, damaged, or tampered with.

```text
Original Evidence
        ↓
Tracked Handling
        ↓
Integrity Maintained
```

---

### 2. Track Every Person Who Handled Evidence

Records:

* Who collected it
* Who transferred it
* Who analyzed it
* Who stored it

---

### 3. Maintain Accountability

Every handler is responsible for the evidence while it is in their possession.

---

### 4. Ensure Legal Admissibility

Courts require proof that evidence remained secure throughout the investigation.

Without a proper Chain of Custody:

❌ Evidence may be rejected in court.

---

### 5. Prevent Tampering Allegations

Provides proof that:

* No files were modified
* No data was inserted
* No evidence was removed

---

### 6. Establish Authenticity

Shows that the evidence presented is the same evidence that was originally collected.

---

## One-Line Exam Answer

> **The purpose of Chain of Custody is to maintain the integrity, authenticity, accountability, and legal admissibility of evidence by documenting every person who handles it from collection to presentation.**

## Memory Trick

**I-A-A-L**

* **I** = Integrity
* **A** = Authenticity
* **A** = Accountability
* **L** = Legal Admissibility

These are the four keywords examiners usually expect in answers about Chain of Custody.
