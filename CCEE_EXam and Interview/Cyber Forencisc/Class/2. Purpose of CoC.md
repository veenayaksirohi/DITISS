## Purpose of Chain of Custody

Track the history of evidence from collection to presentation in court.

**Chain of Custody (CoC)** is used to maintain and document the complete history of evidence from the time it is collected until it is presented in court.

### Objectives

* Preserve evidence integrity.
* Maintain evidence authenticity.
* Ensure accountability.
* Prevent tampering or unauthorized access.
* Establish legal admissibility.
* Record every transfer and handling of evidence.

### Information Recorded

* Case Number
* Evidence ID
* Description of Evidence
* Date and Time
* Location
* Collected By
* Received By
* Purpose of Transfer
* Signatures

### Importance

A properly maintained Chain of Custody proves that the evidence presented is the same evidence that was originally collected and has not been altered.

### One-Line Exam Answer

**Chain of Custody is the documented process used to track, preserve, and maintain the integrity of evidence from collection to presentation in court.**
