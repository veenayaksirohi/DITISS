6. Chain of Custody maintains evidence integrity.
7. Hacking is a cyber crime.
8. Unauthorized activity may not always be a crime.
9. IT Act 2000 is India's primary cyber law.
10. Section 66 = Computer-related offenses.
11. Section 66C = Identity Theft.
12. Section 66D = Online cheating/personation.
13. Section 66E = Privacy violation.
14. Identification is the first forensic step.
15. Presentation is the last forensic step.
16. RAM contains volatile data.
17. Live forensics captures volatile evidence.

19. Volatility is used for memory forensics.
